DroneRanger

Autonomous wildlife deterrence, tested in simulation.

IEEE CCWC 2026

This project uses ROS 2 and PX4 to control a drone in Unity and Gazebo simulations. The drone follows wildlife and avoids obstacles to test non-contact deterrence.

My contribution

  • Developed ROS 2 / PX4 offboard control with staged takeoff, hover, and pursuit at 30 Hz, including altitude and movement limits.
  • Implemented stateful obstacle avoidance with blocked-direction memory and recovery behavior.
  • Integrated ROS 2 with PX4 and Unity/Gazebo, including coordinate conversion and DDS interfaces, and co-authored the conference paper.

Climate Shield

Climate alerts and community help requests.

1st place · Silicon Valley campus

Our hackathon team built a web platform for climate alerts and community disaster response. Users can post requests for help, offer help, and read alerts.

What our team built

  • A community feed for official information, requests for help, and offers of assistance.
  • Post categories and filtering to help visitors find relevant resources.
  • AI-assisted summaries of previous disasters and response measures.

The project won first place at Northeastern University’s Silicon Valley campus climate resilience hackathon.

LLM Inference on Kubernetes

Running vLLM on a single-GPU Kubernetes cluster.

I deployed a vLLM service on Kubernetes and documented problems with model startup and GPU scheduling. This is a personal learning project.

What I worked on

  • Served Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct through an OpenAI-compatible API on Kubernetes.
  • Reproduced a restart loop caused by a liveness probe firing before model loading completed, then introduced a startup probe.
  • Investigated a rolling-update deadlock when two pods competed for one GPU, and documented the availability tradeoff of a Recreate deployment strategy.
